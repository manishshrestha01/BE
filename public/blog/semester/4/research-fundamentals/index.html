Introduction

Research Fundamentals in Semester 4 prepares students to execute structured project and research work with academic and professional rigor. In Pokhara University BE Computer Engineering, this course is designed as both a conceptual and practical foundation for proposal development, ethical research conduct, and report writing. It helps students move from idea-level thinking to evidence-based problem-solving with clear methodology and documentation.

The syllabus begins with basics of research, purpose, outcomes, and project orientation. It then introduces a full research process model that includes literature review, question formulation, conceptual framework, strategy selection, data generation, and data analysis. This unit is especially important because it directly shapes how students plan and justify their project work.

The final units cover participant rights, research ethics, proposal/report structure, and presentation practices. Practical work is organized in phases where teams define a problem, prepare proposal, execute the project, and submit final report with oral presentation. Students who follow this process carefully develop strong technical communication, methodological clarity, and final-year project readiness.

What you will learn

  • Differentiate research purpose, aim, objectives, and expected outcomes.
  • Apply a structured research process model from motivation to analysis.
  • Conduct literature review with proper citation and referencing.
  • Formulate research questions and conceptual frameworks.
  • Choose suitable research strategies and data-generation methods.
  • Understand participant rights, research law, and ethical responsibilities.
  • Write complete research proposals and project reports.
  • Present project progress and final outcomes in professional academic format.

Syllabus Overview

Unit 1: Introduction (4 hrs)

  • What is research and core research terminology.
  • Research aim and objectives.
  • Features and types of research.
  • The 6Ps of research.
  • Purpose and product of research.
  • Research and project relationship.

Unit 2: Research Process Model (10 hrs)

  • Personal experience and motivation in topic selection.
  • Literature review: purpose, resources, execution, and citation.
  • Bibliographic details, referencing systems, and plagiarism awareness.
  • Research question formulation.
  • Conceptual framework development.
  • Research strategies: survey, design and creation, experiment, case study, action research, ethnography.
  • Data generation methods: interview, observation, questionnaire, and documents.
  • Triangulation types in research projects.
  • Quantitative and qualitative data analysis basics.

Unit 3: Participants and Research Ethics (4 hrs)

  • Participants in research settings.
  • Law and research.
  • Rights of people directly involved.
  • Responsibilities of an ethical researcher.

Unit 4: Proposal Writing (4 hrs)

  • What is a research proposal.
  • Need for proposal writing.
  • Core components of a research proposal.
  • Case study on research paper/project proposal.

Unit 5: Report Writing (4 hrs)

  • What is a research report.
  • Need for report writing.
  • Core components of a research report.
  • Case study on research paper/report structure.

Important Topics

  • Research aim-objective-problem alignment.
  • Literature review quality and source credibility.
  • Plagiarism prevention and referencing discipline.
  • Conceptual framework and strategy selection.
  • Data collection validity and triangulation.
  • Qualitative vs quantitative analysis choices.
  • Participant rights and ethical approval mindset.
  • Proposal structure with clear methodology and timeline.
  • Report writing flow from introduction to findings.
  • Oral presentation of progress and final outcomes.

Practice Questions

  1. Define a research problem and write clear aim and objectives for it.
  2. Prepare a short literature review with proper citation and references.
  3. Formulate research questions and a conceptual framework for a selected topic.
  4. Choose one strategy (survey/experiment/case study) and justify its suitability.
  5. Design a basic data-generation plan using at least two methods.
  6. Identify ethical risks in a study and propose mitigation steps.
  7. Draft a proposal outline including methodology, timeline, and expected outcomes.
  8. Prepare a final report structure and a brief presentation script for project defense.
