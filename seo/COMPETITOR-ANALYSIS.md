## Where StudyMate wins today
- Only player with **per-chapter pages** (title/H1 per unit), full syllabus depth, course
  schema potential, OG images, sitemap, college pages, and a native app.
- Original subpoint content from the official 2022 curriculum (first-party data) vs. raw
  Drive links everywhere else.
- Cross-purposed: it quietly serves the **NEC/Nepal Engineering License** audience too.

## Gaps to exploit (they are all thin on answers)
1. **Nobody answers questions.** All competitors are link lists. Chapter pages that *answer*
   "define X", "difference between X and Y", "PU asked this in 2022" win both Google featured
   snippets and AI citations.
2. **Course/College/LearningResource + FAQ schema is absent** across the field → organic
   rich-result + GEO opportunity.
3. **AI crawlers see nothing.** Their content is empirical JS+Drive or PDF; GPTBot sees the
   wrapper, not the answer. StudyMate's SSR'd HTML answers become citable.
4. **llms.txt** — no competitor ships it.
5. **App + blog + college pages** combine intent layers competitors can't match (exam season,
   per-college internal exams, old questions).

## Overlap of target keywords vs. field
- "pokhara university notes", "pokhara university computer engineering notes",
  "pu be notes", "be computer notes semester 1..8" → everywhere + low-quality (WIN).
- "pokhara university old questions / model questions" → wapnepal + soe + studocu (WIN with
  /pu-exam + per-subject old-question pages).
- "csit notes" / "bca notes pu" → adjacent TU giants (csitnoteshub) — evaluate as a Phase-2
  authored expansion, not a fight today.
- "ncit / gces / himalaya notes 'pokhara'", "engineering license exam nepal" → nobody owns
  the combination (WIN if college pages + license section are built).

## Conclusion
Beat the field on **answers + structured data + AI-readability**, not on link volume. The
official domain (pu.edu.np) we out-format; the aggregators we out-depth; csitnoteshub is the
UX benchmark to match in-page.
